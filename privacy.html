Privacy policy

Gude Rush · updated November 12, 2026 · Português

Gude Rush is a marble race game for Android. This page explains what happens to your data when you use the game.

No data is collected

The game does not collect, send or share any personal or usage data. It has no sign-up, no ads, no purchases, no analytics tools and no internet access.

What stays on your device

Your settings (volumes, language, vibration, track theme and the other options), the tracks you build and save, and your marble customizations (names, looks and size) are stored only on your device. The game does not send this data anywhere and does not include it in Android's automatic backup.

To erase it, use the delete buttons on each screen (saved tracks, customizations and personal marbles) or uninstall the game.

Personal marbles (images)

In Customize marbles, you can create a marble from an image of your own. You choose the image in Android's own file picker: the game only receives the image you pick, and only when you pick it. It is cropped into a marble on the device, and the game keeps only that small marble, in its private storage on the device. The original image is not copied, and nothing leaves the device.

Motion sensor

To mix the marbles in the jar, the game reads the phone's accelerometer while you shake it. This reading is used on the spot and is neither stored nor sent.

Permissions

The game only uses the vibration permission, so the phone vibrates at moments of the race. It can be turned off in the game's Settings. The game does not ask for access to the internet, location, contacts, camera or your whole photo gallery.

Children

Since no data is collected, the game does not collect data from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this same page, with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer's contact e-mail, on the Gude Rush page on Google Play.
